# Agon Control — film script

## Director's treatment

- **Promise:** Agon makes endpoint management legible: one operator console, one transparent agent, a narrow and visible action boundary.
- **Identity:** Palette inferred from the app UI, not an official brand guide: ink/blue-black, acid-lime, soft silver, a restrained amber warning. Display face Space Grotesk; technical labels JetBrains Mono.
- **Hero:** A luminous relay path connecting a control-plane console to a visibly consenting endpoint agent.
- **Motion:** Network-line stroke tracing; interface panels assemble from offset planes; a hard aperture/push-through transition carries the lime relay node between shots.
- **Arc:** Start with a singular connection idea, inspect the relay mechanism, reveal real boundaries and current enrollment state, finish with a composed system map and truthful status.
- **Payoff:** The end card resolves the topology into a clear statement and explicitly says no laptop is enrolled yet; it is not a false live-system victory lap.
- **Format:** 16:9 landscape, 24 seconds.

## Full shot script

### Shot 01 / 0.00–5.15s / 5.15s — “ONE CONTROL PLANE”

**Purpose:** Make the central relationship immediate: an operator console can coordinate a transparent endpoint agent through a relay.

**Exact content and holds:** At 0.18s: “AGON / SERVER CONTROL” in a small mono eyebrow at upper-left. At 0.55s: “One control plane.” in large display type left of center. At 1.55s: “Console” under the left node. At 2.25s: “Hosted relay” beside the center node. At 3.00s: “Endpoint agent” under the right node. Hold all labels until 4.40s. No VO.

**Composition:** Ink field with thin technical coordinate marks. The large phrase owns left-center, 9vmin; network nodes occupy the right 55%, with an oversized relay orb centered at 69% x / 50% y. Safe inset 6%. A faint concentric calibration circle sits behind nodes. Acid-lime is reserved for the live route; silver labels stay secondary.

**Artistic idea:** One moving lime pulse draws the topology; the pulse itself becomes the carrier into the next shot.

**Timed choreography:** 0.00–0.22s: small lime node snaps into view with a stiff spring; eyebrow is already on screen. 0.40s: console frame clicks in from two offset planes. 0.55–1.15s: headline reveals through a vertical mask. 1.25–2.00s: first route traces from console to relay; relay disc contracts then blooms. 2.05–2.85s: route extends to endpoint; endpoint ring assembles from quarter arcs. 3.00–3.65s: three mono callouts arrive sequentially, not stacked. 3.65–4.35s: pulse traverses the entire route once. 4.35–5.15s: camera pushes into the center pulse, which grows to a square of lime with eased blur to carry into Shot 02.

**Transition contract:** Lime pulse is the same object in Shot 02: same center position, same color, growing into a broad signal bar while the outgoing topology shears away.

**Assets and technique:** Custom SVG network geometry, CSS coordinate marks, Framer Motion stroke tracing and transform/opacity. No external imagery; these are product diagrams, not a physical product.

**Sound intent:** Instrumental electronic bed. Aim the relay pulse and outgoing push at the nearest actual generated onset; timing initially unverified.

### Shot 02 / 5.15–10.85s / 5.70s — “A RELAY, NOT A TUNNEL”

**Purpose:** Explain that the agent makes an outbound connection to a hosted relay, without implying a live endpoint is already enrolled.

**Exact content and holds:** At 5.30s: “OUTBOUND • ENCRYPTED TRANSPORT” in mono top-left. At 5.80s: “Agent ↔ relay” in display type, 7vmin. At 6.90s: “heartbeat” beside the moving pulse. At 7.80s: “inventory.refresh” on a packet tag. At 8.70s: “agent.ping” on a second packet tag. At 9.65s: “No laptop enrolled yet” on a small amber-bordered state label. Hold this status through 10.70s. No VO.

**Composition:** Start with lime pulse filling the center, then pull back to a broad diagonal route crossing a dark technical field. At 7.0s the composition resolves into a sliced product UI: a right-heavy console pane and left-side endpoint silhouette/agent badge, with packet labels floating along the route. The amber state tag lives at lower-right, away from the headline.

**Artistic idea:** The pulse becomes a relay envelope; its interior transforms into the agent's visible connection handshake. A broken/dashed endpoint contour and explicit pending label keep the distinction between architecture and current live enrollment honest.

**Timed choreography:** 0.00–0.35s: the carried pulse unblurs and stretches into a fast horizontal rail. 0.35–1.05s: camera pulls out; relay mark assembles around the rail. 1.10–2.05s: a packet moves from endpoint side toward relay; thin echo lines trail behind it. 1.60s: heading masks on from the top. 2.10–3.10s: two small packet tags attach sequentially to the moving pulse. 3.15–4.05s: relay envelope flips in perspective; its internal route becomes a UI status panel. 4.05–4.65s: amber “No laptop enrolled yet” state enters with a short underline trace. 4.65–5.30s: the signal expands into a lime rectangular aperture, pushing the UI panel toward camera. 5.30–5.70s: next shot's UI begins to appear inside the aperture before the cut resolves.

**Transition contract:** The aperture is the UI's top-left lime status pip in Shot 03; it lands at matching scale while the rest of the dense panel unfolds around it.

**Assets and technique:** SVG rails and packet paths, HTML/CSS UI panels, masked type, perspective flip, rectangular clip-path aperture.

**Sound intent:** Continue instrumental. Target packet tags and aperture to measured musical accents; exact alignment unverified until bed analysis.

### Shot 03 / 10.85–18.10s / 7.25s — “A SMALL, VISIBLE ALLOWLIST”

**Purpose:** Show the useful operational surface while making clear that arbitrary shell and remote desktop are not implied capabilities.

**Exact content and holds:** At 11.00s: “ACTIONS WITH BOUNDARIES” in mono eyebrow. At 11.30s: a console title “Support actions” in the product-panel art. Rows appear at 12.00s “Agent ping”, 12.90s “Inventory refresh”, 13.80s “Network diagnostics”. At 14.70s show a crossed-out row “Arbitrary shell — not available”. At 15.40s show an amber pill “Endpoint approval may be required”. At 16.10s: “Not remote desktop.” in large display type. Hold content through 17.85s. No VO.

**Composition:** Dark ink interface plane fills most of the frame, scaled/cropped as though the camera inspects a real console. Rows stack vertically in the right 60%; left third is a lime outline word “ALLOWLIST” at 8vmin and the headline zone. Thin corner brackets and a fixed vertical divider define the screen. Warm amber is used only for the approval caveat.

**Artistic idea:** The visible boundary draws itself around three valid actions, then physically blocks an arbitrary-shell row with a crisp crossbar. The line becomes a perimeter ring, never a misleading “remote session” window.

**Timed choreography:** 0.00–0.45s: carried status pip locks into the cropped panel; two UI planes rotate into alignment. 0.45–1.20s: eyebrow and “ALLOWLIST” reveal in separate masks. 1.15–2.00s: first action row assembles from a line and a dot. 2.00–2.80s: second row pulses once as its icon resolves. 2.80–3.60s: third row arrives; connecting rule traces the shared boundary. 3.65–4.45s: disabled arbitrary-shell row appears beneath the boundary; a crossbar snaps through it. 4.55–5.25s: amber approval caveat enters as an attached tag. 5.25–6.05s: panel recedes; “Not remote desktop.” expands in the left area while rows remain readable in the background. 6.05–6.65s: the boundary line lifts off the screen plane and curves into a wide orbit. 6.65–7.25s: orbit rotates into the final map's first ring.

**Transition contract:** The ring from the allowlist perimeter remains the same orbit around the overall system in Shot 04; panel dissolves by a two-plane fold while the ring remains continuous.

**Assets and technique:** CSS/HTML native interface artwork, SVG tracing line, restrained perspective and panel masks; no interactivity or fake working controls.

**Sound intent:** Instrumental only. Keep a clean, percussive transition when the unavailable action is crossed out; use nearest measured onset.

### Shot 04 / 18.10–24.00s / 5.90s — “VISIBLE. BOUNDED. PENDING.”

**Purpose:** Resolve the system architecture and truthfully separate implemented capability from current deployment state.

**Exact content and holds:** At 18.22s: “AGON” in a typographic wordmark, not a fabricated logo. At 18.60s: “Visible. Bounded. Pending.” large, centered-left, 8vmin. At 19.65s: “Hosted relay + consent-based endpoint agent” in supporting copy. At 20.65s: status card: “CONNECTION STATUS / No laptop enrolled yet”. At 21.65s: small footer “Diagnostics are allowlisted. Arbitrary shell and full remote desktop are not available.” Keep all copy through 23.65s; transition begins 23.25s.

**Composition:** Palette inverts from the preceding dense panel to a warm pale mineral field with dark ink typography. System map is smaller and centered on the right half; the left half is the clean editorial lockup. The status card is amber-accented, not green. The perimeter orbit from Shot 03 encloses both console and endpoint nodes, with the endpoint dashed and not shown as live.

**Artistic idea:** Rather than a triumphant green check, the final state uses an amber outline and incomplete endpoint contour. The complete architecture is legible, but a visibly open segment makes the missing enrollment the visual payoff.

**Timed choreography:** 0.00–0.55s: carried ring expands into full orbit as the UI plane folds out. 0.35–1.10s: palette flips to pale mineral through a two-panel split. 0.65–1.35s: “AGON” and headline resolve on the left, no rolling type. 1.35–2.15s: console and relay nodes assemble on the right; endpoint node arrives dashed. 2.15–3.00s: supporting copy resolves beneath title. 3.00–3.70s: amber status card appears and its open-circuit symbol traces to “No laptop enrolled yet”. 3.70–4.50s: limitation line reveals, while orbit settles behind the system map. 4.50–5.05s: headline shifts up 1vmin and the map contracts into the same lime node shape and center position as Shot 01's opener. 5.05–5.90s: node travels toward frame center as an outgoing zoom/blur; a tiny coordinate reticle reappears beneath it. At 24.00s the node is at the opening's initial point, creating a loop handoff.

**Transition contract:** Loop handoff matches the lime node at the opening's position and scale, with the same reticle coordinates. Background changes back to ink at the cut while the node remains continuous.

**Assets and technique:** Pure motion graphics and interface diagrams. Palette inversion by split panel; SVG orbit, dashed endpoint and open-circuit icon; no fake logo asset or unsupported claims.

**Sound intent:** Instrumental resolves at 24.00s; no VO/SFX requested. Final accent and opening handoff alignment to be set after examining generated track onsets.

## Composition and accuracy review

Adjacent shots vary their dominant media, scale, and layout: orbit/network field; macro relay rail; cropped interface panel; open editorial end-card map. The film distinguishes architecture from live deployment throughout. All agent actions shown are supported by the documented allowlist: `agent.ping`, `inventory.refresh`, `diagnostics.network`, with `session.request` omitted because consent/policy is conditional and less legible here. It does not depict arbitrary shell or full remote desktop as available. The end card is a truthful pending state, not a successful live connection.
